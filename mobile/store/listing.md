## Full description
Freedom Hustle is a set of city guides for people who work from anywhere - written by Arni and Valeria from the time they've spent living and working in each city.

Every guide follows the same practical structure, so you can land and get set up fast:

• First 24 Hours - airport to set-up in the right order, with checklists you can tick off as you go
• Best Areas to Stay - the neighbourhoods compared, and exactly where we stayed
• Monthly Budget - what a month actually cost us
• Cafés, Coworking Spaces, Restaurants and Nightlife
• Gyms & Wellness, Getting Around, Visa & Immigration
• Trips & Activities, Top 10 Things to Experience
• Mistakes to Avoid and our Digital Nomad Toolkit

Every place we recommend comes with a Google Maps link, and our favourites are marked so you know exactly where we'd go.

Live now: Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Da Nang - with more cities across Asia on the way.

Create a free account to unlock every guide. Guides are kept up to date, and new content appears in the app automatically.
