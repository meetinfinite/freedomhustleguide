Chiang Mai runs at a different speed. Mornings belong to the mountain -
Doi Suthep before the tour buses, mist still on the road - afternoons to
cafés, and evenings to night markets where dinner costs less than your
flat white did. It's a city you settle into rather than conquer.

The ten below are how we'd spend a first couple of weeks here: the
mountain temple at sunrise, the old city on foot, a proper khao soi
hunt, the Sunday Walking Street before the crowds peak. Each one comes
with the map or booking card to act on it.
