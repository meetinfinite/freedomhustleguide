Bangkok always felt like more than one city to us. So big, so layered,
so full of different rhythms that it sometimes feels like several
cities living inside one. Maybe that's why Bangkok stays in your memory
differently - not as one place, but as hundreds of small scenes you
keep replaying years later. Bangkok is never one city.

That's what this Top Ten is for - the experiences that let you feel
each of those layers. Whether you're here for a long weekend or a few
months, these ten are how Bangkok tells its story: the fight night that
shows you its intensity, the temple morning that shows you its calm,
the river at sunset, the markets that come alive after dark. Cover
these and you won't just have seen Bangkok - you'll understand just how
many layers it has.

Each one comes with the map or booking card to act on it, so the plan
is one tap away.

The full ten lands in your guide automatically with the next update -
nothing to re-buy, nothing to do. In the meantime,
[Trips & Activities](/guides/bangkok/app/trips-and-activities) covers
the classic Bangkok outings.
