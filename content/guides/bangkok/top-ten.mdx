Whether you're here for a long weekend or a few months, these ten are
how Bangkok tells its story - the fight night that shows you its
intensity, the temple morning that shows you its calm, the river at
sunset, the markets that come alive after dark. Cover these and you
won't just have seen Bangkok - you'll understand it.

Each one comes with the map or booking card to act on it, so the plan
is one tap away.

The full ten lands in your guide automatically with the next update -
nothing to re-buy, nothing to do. In the meantime,
[Trips & Activities](/guides/bangkok/app/trips-and-activities) covers
the classic Bangkok outings.
