Bangkok is a city of extremes, and that's the fun of it. Gold temples at
sunrise, a rooftop sixty floors up by sunset, and the best meal of your
month served for 60 baht on a plastic stool in between. You don't tick
Bangkok off - you let it wear you out in the best way.

The ten below are how we'd spend a first couple of weeks here: a Muay
Thai fight night at the stadium, the Grand Palace and Wat Pho done
properly in one morning, Wat Arun glowing from across the river, a
night market where the crowd is actually local. Each one comes with the
map or booking card to act on it.

The full ten is landing in the next update - it arrives in your guide
automatically, nothing to re-buy. In the meantime,
[Trips & Activities](/guides/bangkok/app/trips-and-activities) covers
the classic Bangkok outings.
